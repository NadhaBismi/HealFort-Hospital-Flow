Dataset: 222 ED visits embedded in this page (length of stay and triage acuity). Method: seeded split into 155 calibration and 67 validation records. Simulated patients are generated from the calibration data only, 30 replications, then compared with the unseen validation records.

What this validates

Error is the gap between simulated and observed values as a percent of the observed value. It is not accuracy. The dataset has no department timestamps, so this checks ED-level length of stay and acuity mix only, not Triage, Lab or Radiology routes. Results change with the split seed because the validation set is small (67 records).
